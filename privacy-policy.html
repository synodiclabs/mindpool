Privacy Policy for Mindpool

Last updated: November 2026

Mindpool ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Synodic Labs, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user data.

1. Data Collection and Local Storage

Mindpool is designed with privacy at its core. Your questions, flashcards, study progress, and personal notes are stored locally on your device using a private SQLite database (Room Persistence Library). We do not operate external proprietary servers to collect, store, or sell your personal learning data.

2. Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

If you choose to use the Cloud Backup feature:

  • Mindpool connects to your Google Account via Google Sign-In with the restricted drive.appdata scope.
  • Backup files are stored exclusively in your personal Google Drive Application Data folder, accessible only by the Mindpool app on your device. We cannot access, view, or read your backup files.

3. Audio Recording Permission (Speech-to-Text)

Mindpool requests access to your device's microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) solely to provide the voice dictation feature for creating questions or notes. Audio data is processed in real time by your device's on-device speech recognizer or Google Speech Services. Mindpool does not record, transmit, or store your raw voice recordings on any server.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Mindpool uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain device data, such as:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • Coarse location and network data
  • Ad interaction metrics

We implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain consent in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other privacy regulations. Users in relevant regions can manage or revoke their advertising consent at any time via Settings > Ad Privacy Choices within the app. You can also upgrade to Mindpool Pro to remove all advertisements permanently.

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases for Mindpool Pro are processed securely through the official Google Play Billing service. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

6. Security

Mindpool provides optional security features such as PIN Protection, Biometric Authentication (fingerprint/facial recognition), and a Privacy Screen feature (blocking screenshots and blurring app content in the recent apps switcher). These features run entirely locally on your device.
